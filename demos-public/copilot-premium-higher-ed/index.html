    Read the comparison assumptions & licensing boundaries
    “Premium” here means the paid Microsoft 365 Copilot organizational experience, not a consumer subscription or a price quote. Product naming, education eligibility, app access, agent limits, and rollout vary. The included baseline shown is standalone Copilot Chat with no attached files or paid organizational agents. It does not provide the same broad cross-Microsoft 365 semantic grounding as the licensed experience.
    Premium examples assume the named sources exist in Microsoft 365, are accessible to the user, and have been indexed; meeting recall requires available meeting artifacts and the appropriate settings. Neither tier inherently connects to your SIS, ERP, grants system, or donor CRM. Approved exports or configured connectors are needed. Researcher and Analyst availability and usage limits must be confirmed. Citations can be wrong; review original sources and resolve conflicts.

    03 / Trust before rollout
    What your CISO and general counsel will ask.
    The purchase does not make an institution compliant. These are the conversations to have before connecting the work.
    
      
    
        
    01 Is our data used to train the model?
    EDP is shared by both tiers
    For eligible organizational use under enterprise data protection, prompts, responses, and Graph data are not used to train foundation models. This is not a Premium-only benefit. EDP is not a promise of zero retention. Web search, agents, and third-party models have additional boundaries and terms.
    Data, privacy & security ↗
    
        
    02 Where is our data processed?
    Verify for your environment
    Storage residency and model processing location are different questions. Review your tenant geography, contractual commitments, EU Data Boundary applicability, selected models, and agent providers. Do not assume every request stays in your country or that all subprocessors share the same boundaries.
    Residency & processing boundaries ↗
    
        
    03 Can we use it with student records?
    FERPA · legal review required
    FERPA is not a product certification. Counsel must assess the permitted use, school-official exception where applicable, legitimate educational interest, contractual controls, retention, and redisclosure. Minimize data, restrict access, and keep humans accountable. Do not upload identifiable student records just because a prompt asks for context.
    U.S. Department of Education: FERPA ↗
    
        
    04 Does it expose overshared files?
    Verify for your environment
    Copilot respects existing permissions; it does not fix excessive permissions. Review SharePoint sites, broad sharing links, guest access, sensitivity labels, DLP, and connector scopes. Assign licenses deliberately and manage agents, web grounding, and available models. Specific controls depend on licenses and configuration.
    Microsoft Purview controls ↗
    
        
    05 Can we audit and discover interactions?
    Verify for your environment
    Microsoft Purview supports auditing, retention, and eDiscovery for supported Copilot interactions. Coverage, event detail, retention periods, and advanced features depend on workload and licensing. Test a prompt-to-audit-to-legal-hold workflow with records management; do not assume deleted chat history evades retention.
    Audit, retention & eDiscovery ↗
    
        
    06 What about GCC?
    Verify for your environment
    Government-cloud offerings exist, but GCC, GCC High, and DoD are not interchangeable. Availability and feature timing differ from commercial tenants. Confirm the exact SKU, education eligibility, meeting features, Researcher/Analyst access, web grounding, and cloud-specific terms before using this gallery as a rollout commitment.

    04 / Earn the right to scale
    A 90-day pilot. Not a campus-wide leap.
    
      
    DAYS 01—30
    Establish the baseline.
    Select 12–20 volunteers across 2–3 offices with repeatable document-heavy tasks. Include accessibility needs, a skeptic, and one champion per office.
    IT and counsel approve sources, permissions, and data-handling rules. Time at least 10 comparable tasks per cohort, including retrieval and review.
    Exit evidenceApproved data scope, baseline task time, quality rubric, named pilot owner.
    DAYS 31—60
    Measure the whole workflow.
    Champions run weekly office hours and share reviewed prompt pairs in Teams. Compare matched tasks with included Chat and Premium; track checking and correction time.
    Measure median end-to-end time, citation validity, rework, weekly active use, and accessibility issues. Record failures, not just successes.
    Exit evidenceWeekly scorecard, reviewed examples, incident log, updated realization rate.
    DAYS 61—90
    Scale only what earns it.
    Finance validates recovered capacity and its redeployment. CISO and counsel review exceptions. Faculty and staff champions publish role-specific playbooks.
    Agree thresholds before deciding: for example, 20% lower median task time, no quality regression, and zero unresolved high-risk data incidents. These are pilot goals, not promises.
    Exit evidenceScale / refine / stop decision, full-cost model, accountable service owner.
