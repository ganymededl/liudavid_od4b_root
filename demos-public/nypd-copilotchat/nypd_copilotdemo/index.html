Prompt 2

Explain a NYS statute in plain English before a citizen contact

Explain NYS Vehicle and Traffic Law Section 1111(d) — failure to stop at a steady red signal — in plain English, as if briefing a new officer at roll call. Tell me:
1. What the violation actually is, in one sentence.
2. The elements that have to be present to issue a summons.
3. Two or three common real-world examples in a NYC setting.
4. One thing officers frequently get wrong about it.

Keep it under 200 words.

What it does

Gives the officer a quick, plain-language refresher on a NYS statute so they're confident explaining it to a motorist or pedestrian.

What good output looks like

A four-part answer under 200 words: one-sentence definition, bulleted elements, concrete NYC examples, and a common pitfall — readable in under a minute.

Prompt 3

Prepare respectful talking points for a sensitive community interaction

Give me five short talking points I can use to respectfully explain to a resident why I can't share details about an ongoing investigation on their block. The resident is worried about their family's safety. Tone should be calm, empathetic, and non-confrontational. Each talking point should be one or two sentences, plain spoken, no legal jargon.

What it does

Prepares an officer with ready-to-say language for a hard doorstep conversation — acknowledging concern without compromising the case.

What good output looks like

Five short, human talking points an officer could actually say out loud: acknowledges the concern, explains the limit plainly, offers a next step, and never sounds scripted.

What Copilot Chat will not do with the Basic license

With the Copilot Chat Basic license only — no add-ons — the boundaries below describe where Copilot Chat stops on its own and where supervisory or Legal Bureau review takes over.

Copilot Chat is a general-purpose reasoning and writing assistant protected by Enterprise Data Protection. It is not grounded in your systems, and this prompt set is intentionally scoped to internal-first artifacts that go through normal review before anything external happens.

What Copilot Chat will not do with the license alone

Won't read an officer's Outlook mailbox or calendar
Won't read Teams chats, channels, or meeting transcripts
Won't search or summarize SharePoint or OneDrive files automatically
Won't connect to CAD, RMS, DAS, eSummons, or any case-management system
Won't retrieve real-time crime data, CompStat numbers, or arrest records (public web data still accessible)
Won't draft public-facing communications involving named complainants, victims, or subjects (not a system constraint, governance/policy decision)
Won't produce arrest reports, complaint reports, or use-of-force statements (not a system constraint, model can generate this content)
Won't perform probable cause determinations or disciplinary documentation (not a system constraint, advisory limitation)
Won't replace supervisory or Legal Bureau review of any report before it's filed or sent (not a system constraint, human oversight requirement)
Won't retain your prompts to train foundation models (EDP protection)
