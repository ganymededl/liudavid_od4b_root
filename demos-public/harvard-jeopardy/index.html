Rules & Controls

  1. Select a value: read the clue, type a response, then reveal it for the presenter’s ruling.
  2. Scoring: Correct adds the clue value; Incorrect subtracts it. Negative scores appear in red.
  3. Daily Double: one hidden tile is randomized per game. Wager $5 through the greater of your score or $1,000 before seeing its clue.
  4. Final: after the category reveal, wager from $0 through your positive score. You have 30 seconds once the clue appears.
  5. Keyboard: Tab navigates controls; arrow keys move among board tiles; Enter/Space selects; Escape closes Rules.
