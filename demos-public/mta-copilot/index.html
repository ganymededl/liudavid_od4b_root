What Copilot Chat will not do with the Basic license

With the Copilot Basic license only — no add-ons — the boundaries below describe where Copilot Chat stops on its own and where supervisory or governance review takes over.

Copilot Chat is a general-purpose reasoning and writing assistant protected by Enterprise Data Protection. It is not grounded in your systems, and this prompt set is intentionally scoped to internal-first artifacts that go through normal review before anything external happens.

What Copilot Chat will not do with the license alone

Won't read an employee's Outlook mailbox or calendar
Won't read Teams chats, channels, or meeting transcripts
Won't search or summarize SharePoint or OneDrive files automatically
Won't connect to PeopleSoft, Maximo, EAM, finance ledgers, or procurement systems
Won't retrieve internal real-time service status, ridership, or asset telemetry (public web data still accessible)
Won't draft customer complaint responses involving named riders (not a system constraint, governance/policy decision)
Won't produce incident reports, accident reports, or operator statements (not a system constraint, model can generate this content)
Won't perform root-cause determinations or disciplinary documentation (not a system constraint, advisory limitation)
Won't replace supervisory or legal review of any artifact before it's filed or sent (not a system constraint, human oversight requirement)
Won't retain your prompts to train foundation models (EDP protection)
