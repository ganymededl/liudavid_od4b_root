What Copilot Chat will not do with the Basic license

With the Copilot Chat Basic license only — no add-ons — the boundaries below describe where Copilot Chat stops on its own and where supervisory, HR, or legal review takes over.

Copilot Chat is a general-purpose reasoning and writing assistant protected by Enterprise Data Protection. It is not grounded in your SIS, LMS, or HRIS, and this prompt set is intentionally scoped to internal-first drafts that go through normal review before anything goes to a family, board, or external party.

What Copilot Chat will not do with the license alone

Won’t read a staff member’s Outlook mailbox or calendar
Won’t read Teams chats, channels, or meeting transcripts
Won’t search or summarize SharePoint or OneDrive files automatically
Won’t connect to PowerSchool, Banner, Workday, Canvas, or any SIS/LMS/HRIS
Won’t retrieve real-time grade data, attendance records, or IEP files (public web data still accessible)
Won’t draft public-facing communications about named students, families, or staff (not a system constraint, FERPA & governance decision)
Won’t produce final disciplinary letters, IEP documents, or HR investigation findings (not a system constraint, model can draft, humans must finalize)
Won’t make admission, eligibility, or financial aid determinations (not a system constraint, advisory limitation)
Won’t replace HR, Title IX, or counsel review of any sensitive draft before it’s sent (not a system constraint, human oversight requirement)
Won’t retain your prompts to train foundation models (EDP protection)
