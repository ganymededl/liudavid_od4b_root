Lead story

Cedar Valley — less waiting, more serving

30% faster intake in a four-week pilot gives Cedar Valley a practical path to expand resident services.

Background. Staff were re-entering the same requests across disconnected queues. The team chose one high-volume workflow and agreed on a baseline before changing it.

The win. The pilot now routes requests to the right owner automatically. Two departments are ready for the next rollout.

What's next. Taylor will confirm the expansion plan and accessibility review by September 28.
