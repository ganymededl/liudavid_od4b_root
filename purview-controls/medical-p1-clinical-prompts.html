What was tested

  1. The custom detector matched DIAXMRN-48276105 when the supporting MRN wording was present.
  2. A short value, DIAXMRN-1234, did not match.
  3. A full-length value without the supporting keyword did not match.
  4. The first Copilot Chat attempt before enforcement propagation was not blocked.
  5. A genuine retry at 14:08 with the MRN wording was blocked with the organization's policy message.
  6. A 14:15 negative control containing a full synthetic identifier but no MRN keyword was allowed.
  7. Activity Explorer later showed the P1 rule match for the positive prompt; the negative control had no P1 match.
  8. T8a, run as a user outside the Testers group, was allowed without a policy banner.

Evidence screenshots

Evidence limitation: no dedicated screenshot of the genuine 14:08 T1 block was saved. The block text was observed in the tested surface and the P1 attribution was confirmed in Activity Explorer. This page does not substitute the earlier invalid 12:50 screenshot claim.

What this result does and does not establish

Supported by the lab

  • The custom MRN detector required both the pattern and supporting clinical wording.
  • The scoped P1 policy matched the positive prompt and did not match the negative control.
  • The non-Testers control behaved as expected.

Still tenant-dependent

  • The detector is synthetic and is not a comprehensive PHI classifier.
  • Policy propagation delayed the first test.
  • Licensing, roles, and enforcement behavior must be validated in the target tenant.
