0What is Cowork?

  • A scheduler fires a prompt on a cadence you set (once, daily, weekly, on-demand).
  • Copilot Cowork runs the prompt with your M365 data in reach — mail, Teams, files, calendar, SharePoint.
  • A file artifact lands in output/ — a docx, pptx, xlsx, PDF, or HTML — ready to share or review.
Every Cowork artifact is only as good as the prompt that drove it. The next 5 panels are the prompt.

1Role + lens

Tell Copilot Cowork who it is and what expertise to bring. "Senior Microsoft CSA for US Federal" produces different output than "Generic assistant." Specify the job title, the domain, and the viewpoint it should take.

0 words
Weak"You are a helpful assistant that helps with customer work."
Strong"You are a senior Microsoft Cloud Solutions Architect supporting US SLED customers, with deep expertise in Azure governance, Entra ID, and Purview. Your lens is risk-first: always surface compliance exposure before optimization opportunities."

2Input spec

Spell out what data Copilot Cowork is working with — file paths in input/, M365 sources (email, Teams, calendar), specific docs, time ranges. Ambiguity here is the #1 cause of wrong outputs.

0 words
Weak"Look at the customer file and recent emails."
Strong"Use (1) input/customer-asks.xlsx — columns: Feature, Priority, Date_Requested; (2) my last 60 days of Outlook mail where subject contains 'GCC' or 'parity'; (3) the Microsoft 365 Roadmap export at input/roadmap.csv. Ignore anything older than 6 months."

3Output spec

Describe the exact deliverable — format (markdown, docx, HTML), structure (sections, columns, bullet counts), length, and any required headers or summaries. If you want a table, name the columns.

0 words
Weak"Summarize the gaps in a nice format."
Strong"A markdown table sorted by Gap descending. Columns: Feature, Commercial_GA, GCC_GA, GCC_High_GA, Gap_Days, Customer_Impact (H/M/L), Workaround. Precede the table with a 2-sentence executive summary. Max 25 rows."

4Constraints

Explicit rules about what to exclude, avoid, or flag. Constraints are where most "polished" prompts fail — a strong constraint list prevents hallucinated data, sensitive info leakage, and format drift.

0 words
Weak"Keep it clean and professional."
Strong"Exclude preview/beta features. Never include internal Microsoft-confidential pricing. Flag any feature where GCC date is TBD with ⚠. Do not speculate on unannounced roadmap items — if the source doesn't have a date, write 'Not announced.' Avoid competitor comparisons."

5Audience + use context

Who reads this, where, and when. "CIO on a phone, 10 minutes before the meeting" drives very different tone and density than "Engineering team reviewing async in Teams over a week." Name the reader, the device, and the moment.

0 words
Weak"For internal stakeholders."
Strong"Federal customer CIO reviewing on a laptop during a quarterly business review. Will copy-paste sections into an email to their security team. Skimmable in 90 seconds; no Microsoft jargon the customer hasn't already used in prior meetings."
