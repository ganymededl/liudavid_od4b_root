Alan Turing / album announcement

Pet Shop Boys have announced A man from the future, an album exploring Alan Turing's life and work, for October 9, 2026 via Parlophone. It revises the work first performed at the BBC Proms in 2014, with narration by Juliet Stevenson and contributions from Andrew Hodges, the Manchester Camerata Octet, and Sven Helbig.
