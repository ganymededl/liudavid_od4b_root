    Behind the instrument: provenance, model, and production boundary

    Sources: Personal Hub index and public repository paths inspected on October 17, 2026. Private Q&A and parity-tool descriptions are taken from their Hub cards only; their underlying content was not accessed. A source's existence does not establish quality, currency, compatibility, or customer authorization.

    Model: Deterministic, inspectable planning rules, not an AI recommendation service. Base stage hours are discovery 2, controls 3, prompt design 2, rehearsal 3, evidence 3, and measurement 2; transit rehearsal adds 1. Scenario allowances add 2 hours of cloud validation for GCC or 4 for GCC High; these are arbitrary planning assumptions, not statements about product availability. Feature loss adds 2 design and 1 rehearsal hours; an audit challenge adds 3 evidence hours. The capacity challenge halves the selected capacity. Checks affect evidence gaps, not the effort estimate. No ROI, usage, price, or savings is inferred.

    Limits: This is a static, local-first prototype. No sign-in, telemetry, cookies, external scripts, local storage, private-data imports, background fetches, tenant queries, or deployments. Exports contain synthetic scenario selections and public references only. Closing or reloading the tab clears the replay and comparison.

    Production path: Keep the public lab synthetic. A separate, authenticated internal edition would need approved data connectors, least-privilege access, tenant and cloud isolation, source freshness and provenance, retention and deletion policy, audited decisions, support ownership, and human approval before any write. Customer evidence must not be added to this public repository.
